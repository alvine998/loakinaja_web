# LoakinAja — Jual beli barang bekas terpercaya

LoakinAja is an Indonesian marketplace for buying and selling quality pre-owned goods, with Rekening Bersama transaction support.

## Browse products

Browse [all active product listings](https://loakinaja.com/category/semua) by category. Product listings include price, condition, location, photos, and seller details.

## Selling and pricing

Sellers use one token to publish one listing. New accounts receive three free tokens. Paid token packages are listed on the public [pricing page](https://loakinaja.com/harga), starting at Rp 5,000 for five tokens.

## Rekening Bersama

Rekening Bersama holds buyer payment until delivery is confirmed. Payment receipt and seller withdrawals are manually verified and processed by LoakinAja administrators.
